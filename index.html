I'm an undergraduate studying physics and mathematics at the University of Pennsylvania. My research interests lie in quantum gravity and holography, particularly the role of quantum entanglement in emergent spacetime geometry.

I'm interested in how semiclassical gravity and bulk locality arise from underlying quantum degrees of freedom through holographic duality and bulk reconstruction.

01 Research

Research experience in quantum field theory, nonequilibrium statistical mechanics, and computational physics.

Feb 2026 – present
University of Pennsylvania

High-energy theory

Senior thesis advisor: Jonathan J. Heckman

Investigating soft photon consistency relations for late-time QED correlators in de Sitter spacetime and their interpretation through generalized one-form symmetries. Developed Schwinger–Keldysh formulations of Ward identities and studied effective descriptions of charged particles using HQET and SCET. Current work examines soft-interaction factorization into Wilson lines.

Mar 2025 – May 2026
University of Pennsylvania

Statistical physics and optimal inference

Advisor: Andrea J. Liu · With Adam Gabriel Frim

Developed a stochastic-thermodynamic framework for sequential inference using finite-state physical memories. Derived weak-signal diffusion limits and a Green–Kubo friction tensor, and formulated optimal update protocols under accuracy and sampling-time constraints using stochastic control and Feynman–Kac methods. Manuscript in preparation.

Apr 2024 – Mar 2025
University of Pennsylvania

Computational particle physics

Advisor: Dylan S. Rankin

Developed a vector-quantized variational autoencoder (VQ-VAE) for unsupervised anomaly detection in ATLAS particle-collision data. Trained classifiers directly on quantized latent representations to assess signal retention without decoder-based reconstruction.

Jan 2022 – Mar 2024
Arizona State University

Computational XFEL research

Advisor: Richard A. Kirian

Developed automated Python methods for characterizing gas dynamic virtual nozzles, including a machine-learning classifier for drip–jet regimes (99.3% accuracy). Contributed particle image velocimetry and statistical analyses of liquid microjets for X-ray free-electron laser experiments.
