Vivek Krishnan

Physics & Mathematics · University of Pennsylvania

I'm an undergraduate studying physics and mathematics at the University of Pennsylvania.

My research interests lie in quantum gravity and holography, particularly how spacetime geometry and gravitational dynamics can emerge from underlying quantum degrees of freedom.

I'm especially interested in the role of quantum entanglement in holographic duality, bulk reconstruction, and the emergence of semiclassical spacetime.
